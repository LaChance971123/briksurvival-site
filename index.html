Power
outage.

When the lights go out.

FIRST CHECKSREAD. THEN ACT.
  1. 01

    Check the situation

    Find the outage map and local emergency alerts. If someone depends on powered medical equipment, use the backup plan and seek urgent help if that equipment cannot run.

  2. 02

    Make the space safe

    Use flashlights rather than candles. Keep refrigerators and freezers closed. Unplug sensitive electronics if power is unstable, and leave one light on to show when service returns.

Water
disruption.

Start with the right water notice.

FIRST CHECKSREAD. THEN ACT.
  1. 01

    Identify the notice

    Find the utility or health department notice for your address, its start time, reason, and permitted uses. Save a screenshot in case service fails.

  2. 02

    Match use to the instruction

    A boil-water notice generally addresses microbes; follow the authority's boil directions. If the notice says do not drink or do not use, follow those stricter limits and the specified alternative-water instructions.

Hurricane.

Know your zone. Know your next move.

FIRST CHECKSREAD. THEN ACT.
  1. 01

    Check your zone

    Read local evacuation and storm-surge instructions for your address, not just the forecast track.

  2. 02

    Leave when directed

    Use official routes to a destination outside the hazard area. Bring medications, identification, pets and phone power.

Home
fire.

Get out. Stay out.

FIRST CHECKSREAD. THEN ACT.
  1. 01

    Leave immediately

    Follow the escape route and use stairs, not elevators. Check doors for heat before opening; use another exit if unsafe.

  2. 02

    If the exit is blocked

    Close doors between you and fire, call emergency services with your location and signal from a window.
