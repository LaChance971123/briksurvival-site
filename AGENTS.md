# Osprey Zero editorial and product mission

Osprey Zero serves lone individuals and families who may lack government assistance, functioning utilities, transport, communication or safe access to officials. Civilian protection and a workable household plan come first. Authorities may be unavailable or implicated in threats; handle this tactfully and without assuming that every official is hostile.

Preserve the dark charcoal, ash and orange identity and the inherited slogan: “Skill beats panic. Every time.”

Every free online guide must lead with a concrete quick answer, immediately followed by relevant references and protective actions. Include a situation-specific fallback when help is unavailable, household/access needs, next decisions, exceptions and source context. Never make calling a hotline the entire action plan. Do not replace specialist assessment, medical training or product-specific advice with invented certainty.

The user has retired all free Osprey Zero PDF downloads, browser offline saving and portable archives. Do not regenerate or publish these. Keep premium manuscripts and finished product files outside this public repository and deployment; Payhip will deliver purchased files. `content/toolkits.json` contains public sales metadata only. Only `ready` products with verified delivery, edition, price and a matching Payhip checkout may show Buy buttons. Never invent a checkout or claim an unreleased package is available. Toolkit CTAs follow the free guide's protective instructions and sources. `offline/sw.js` is only a retirement worker; `app.js` clears only `oz-offline-` caches and the `/offline/` registration, preserving the root advertising worker.

Independent humanitarian and official references are both useful. Label geographic scope, distinguish editorial planning from source-based instructions, and never claim clinical or specialist certification without it. Avoid unverified promises of rescue, safe corridors, shelter, aid or rights. Conflict content remains civilian protective information, not combat, weapons, checkpoint evasion or explosive handling guidance.

Keep a single homepage search. Organize subject → subcategory → guide, with a complete A–Z index and browser-local full-text search. Preserve existing routes. Build structured JSON with scripts/build_library.py; refine preserved pages with scripts/refine_site.py. The publishing output is dist, created by scripts/stage_site.py; do not publish the source checkout.

Homepage, search, urgent emergency-response, privacy and signup confirmation pages must not initialize ad providers. Browsing/preparedness/field pages plus disaster-scams and verify-information automatically load vignette 11941449. Browsing hubs also load push-subscription zone 11941494. That zone is not MultiTag. Never add a MultiTag zone alongside the standalone vignette. Initialise early from ads.js, once per format per document. There is no opt-in gate, ad-off control or site-level session cooldown. Ad-free subscriptions or purchases are future ideas, not implemented features. Push permission is separate; sw.js is the supplied advertising worker, not an offline cache or emergency-alert service.

Before publishing run `python scripts/build_site.py`: library/link checks, search relevance, catalogue/publishing-boundary checks, scoped worker cleanup, JavaScript syntax and ad policy tests. Review representative desktop and mobile behavior. Toolkit and retired-download pages remain ad-free. Do not promise 100% real-world safety, guaranteed ad fill or automatic newsletter delivery; signup collection and email delivery are separate capabilities.
