Resuscitation

Emergency guide

Adult hands-only CPR

If an adult suddenly collapses, is unresponsive and is not breathing normally or is only gasping, call emergency services, get an AED and start chest compressions.

ResuscitationEmergency guide

Child & infant CPR

If a child or infant is unresponsive and not breathing normally, call the local emergency number, begin CPR and get an AED.

ResuscitationEmergency guide

Using an AED

For an unresponsive person who is not breathing normally, call emergency services and begin CPR.

Airway

Emergency guide

Adult & child choking

If someone cannot speak, breathe or cough effectively, call emergency services.

AirwayEmergency guide

Infant choking

For a baby under one year who cannot cry, cough effectively or breathe, request emergency help and alternate 5 back blows with 5 chest thrusts.
