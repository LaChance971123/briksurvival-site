Water

Emergency guide

Boil-water advisory

Use bottled water or bring clear tap water to a rolling boil for 1 minute, then cool it; above 6,500 feet, boil for 3 minutes.

WaterEmergency guide

Do-not-drink advisory

Use commercially bottled or officially approved water for drinking, cooking, brushing teeth, ice, formula and pets.

WaterEmergency guide

Do-not-use advisory

Do not drink or use tap water for any purpose while a do-not-use advisory is in effect.

WaterEmergency guide

Water disruption

After a water outage, damaged pipes or suspected contamination, use sealed bottled or previously stored safe water until you establish the risk.
