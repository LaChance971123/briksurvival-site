Do now.

  1. 01

    Turn it on

    Continue CPR while another helper prepares the AED if possible.

  2. 02

    Expose the chest

    Dry a wet chest and place pads according to the AED diagrams.

  3. 03

    Stand clear

    Make sure no one touches the person during analysis or shock delivery.

  4. 04

    Resume CPR

    Immediately follow the device instructions to continue compressions.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

If nobody can bring an AED, continue CPR rather than leaving the person to search alone. If another person is present, ask them to locate the nearest accessible AED while you continue compressions. Follow the device prompts when it arrives.

People & practical needs.

Give someone the specific task of calling or locating care while another person provides first aid. Keep the patient warm or cool as the condition requires and record what happened and when. Do not put yourself into the same hazard or assume an ambulance has been dispatched until confirmed. For this situation, brief every person on the first action: turn it on. Agree who will manage children, pets or mobility needs while that action happens. For a child or infant, use the appropriate pediatric pads or setting when available and follow the device and dispatcher instructions.

Keep essentials within reach

For using an aed, prioritize only items needed for the first action and the fallback: If nobody can bring an AED, continue CPR rather than leaving the person to search alone. If another person is present, ask them to locate the nearest accessible AED while you continue compressions. Follow the device prompts when it arrives. Keep the relevant checklist and contact information within reach. Equipment retrieval must not delay the protective step.

Next steps.

Keep the AED connected until responders take over. Learn the location of nearby devices and obtain training.

First decision: Turn it on

Continue CPR while another helper prepares the AED if possible. Decide which person can carry this out immediately and what would prevent it in your current location. Do not delay the protective step to complete a household worksheet.

When the original plan fails

If nobody can bring an AED, continue CPR rather than leaving the person to search alone. If another person is present, ask them to locate the nearest accessible AED while you continue compressions. Follow the device prompts when it arrives. Reassess the next available location or resource using the warning signs in this guide; do not assume a promised service is operating.

Before changing location

For a child or infant, use the appropriate pediatric pads or setting when available and follow the device and dispatcher instructions. Check the actual route and the needs of the person who will have the most difficulty moving. A destination is not confirmed until access and conditions have been checked.
