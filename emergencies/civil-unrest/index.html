Civil unrest

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Move away from violence or dangerous crowd movement by a safe route, or shelter in a secure place if movement is riskier. Avoid confrontation, keep family members together and check the actual conditions on your route. Do not assume official presence makes an area safe. If authorities are involved in threats, seek independently verified local or humanitarian help when safe.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Separate the event from your location

    Check what is happening on your route, not just citywide headlines. Peaceful assembly and a nearby dangerous incident are different conditions.

  2. 02

    Choose a safe path

    Move away from active violence and blocked roads. If there is no safe exit, shelter inside, away from windows, and update a trusted contact.

  3. 03

    Stay informed

    Use official local notices for curfews, transport changes, and emergency services, and verify unconfirmed social posts before acting.

  4. 04

    Regroup deliberately

    Arrange a meeting point and a backup contact if mobile networks are strained. Wait for reliable updates before returning to a disrupted area.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Use what you can see safely from your location to judge immediate danger; do not approach a crowd to investigate. Agree on a place to regroup away from the disturbance. If officials or armed personnel are involved in threats, use trusted private contacts or independently verified humanitarian help when safe.

People & practical needs.

Choose two reunion places and an out-of-area contact. Keep children with a trusted caregiver and carry a written contact card. Share sensitive addresses and travel details only with people who need them; avoid posting live locations or images of other families without consent.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, drinking water, a battery light, shoes, phone power and a written contact card within reach. Adapt the load to the hazard and the people with you; do not delay escape to collect equipment.

Next steps.

Before going out, check whether your route crosses a closure and identify an alternative. Carry basic identification, necessary medication, and phone power.

Watch for.

Do not assume a protest is violent or that a citywide alert applies equally to every neighborhood. If an order applies, read its location, hours, and exceptions.
