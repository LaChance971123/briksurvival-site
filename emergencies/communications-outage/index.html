Do now.

  1. 01

    Check alternatives

    Try a safe available landline, another carrier or Wi-Fi calling if configured and functioning.

  2. 02

    Keep messages short

    Send your location, status and next check-in time to your out-of-area contact.

  3. 03

    Protect battery

    Lower brightness, enable low-power mode and limit unnecessary use.

  4. 04

    Use the meeting plan

    If networks fail completely, follow the agreed safe meeting and contact arrangements.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Write a short message with your status, location and next check-in time; try it periodically rather than draining the battery with constant retries. Use your agreed meeting plan only when the route is safe. Do not split up to hunt for signal.

People & practical needs.

Give each person a written contact card and explain what to do if separated. Plan for hearing, vision, language and literacy needs. Keep live family locations and identity details off public posts even when a message seems urgent.

Keep essentials within reach

Prioritize supplies named in this guide that are usable by the person who needs them. Keep the checklist and relevant contact details accessible. Check carrying capacity, storage and equipment instructions in advance; collecting equipment must not delay the immediate protective action.

Next steps.

Keep paper contact lists, offline maps and a radio. Arrange check-in times before a crisis.

Questions in the field.

Is one failed app the whole network?

Try the simplest available channel without repeated battery-draining attempts: a short text, a checked Wi-Fi connection or a prearranged contact. A local internet outage and a cellular outage may require different workarounds; do not assume every service has failed.

What if messages cannot be delivered?

Use the household’s prearranged meeting and check-in plan rather than making unplanned trips to find a signal. Keep a radio or other verified warning source if available. Record unconfirmed information separately from actual contact.

Watch for.

A message sent is not necessarily delivered. Do not assume emergency texting is supported everywhere.
