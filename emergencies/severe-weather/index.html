OZ FIELD GUIDE / 02 / WEATHER

Severe weather

Read the specific warning. The right shelter depends on the hazard.

UPDATED 02 OCT 2026ABOUT 3 MIN READ

THE QUICK ANSWER

Identify the warning for your exact location and act on it now. For a tornado warning, go to a basement or a small interior room on the lowest level, away from windows, and protect your head. For flood danger, move to higher ground and do not enter water. Different hazards require different shelter choices.

!

In immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Current instructions for your location take priority.

01 / DO NOW

First moves.

  1. 01

    Read the warning

    Use local alerts, the National Weather Service, or a weather radio. Check the hazard, affected area, timing, and the action given.

  2. 02

    Choose shelter for the threat

    For tornadoes, move to the lowest interior space in a sturdy building. For flooding, move away from low ground and use the flooding guide. For lightning, go indoors and stay away from windows and plumbing.

  3. 03

    Bring the essentials

    Take shoes, a flashlight, phone, charged battery, medication, and the people and pets with you. Do not delay sheltering to collect a full kit.

  4. 04

    Wait for the all-clear

    A lull does not mean danger is over. Recheck official messages before traveling or inspecting damage.

02 / NEXT

When the first minute is over.

After the warning, avoid downed lines, unstable structures, and flooded roads. Check on others when safe. Review your shelter location and alert settings before the next event.

03 / WATCH FOR

The exception that matters.

A watch means conditions are possible; a warning means the threat is occurring or imminent in the warned area. Do not use a single generic storm plan for tornado, flood, heat, and winter weather.

04 / VERIFY

Sources & context.

Osprey Zero wrote this guide from the references below. It is a general starting point, not a live alert. Check the issuing authority for your area; guidance and conditions can change.
