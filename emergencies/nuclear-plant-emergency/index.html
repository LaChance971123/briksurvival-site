Nuclear power plant emergency

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Listen to the official alert for your zone and follow instructions to shelter or evacuate. If told to shelter, go indoors and close windows and doors. Take potassium iodide only if public-health officials direct it; it is not a general radiation antidote.

Take it with you. Verify locally.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Confirm your zone

    Use emergency management and plant-area alerts for your actual address.

  2. 02

    Shelter when directed

    Move indoors and follow instructions for outside-air ventilation.

  3. 03

    Evacuate when directed

    Use designated routes and reception centers; do not invent a route through restricted areas.

  4. 04

    Follow food and water instructions

    Use packaged supplies and heed any local contamination restrictions.

Next steps.

Keep the local emergency plan, routes and alert channels available before an incident.

Watch for.

Plant emergencies differ from nuclear detonations; a fixed distance or timer is not an all-clear.
