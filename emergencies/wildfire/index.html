Check evacuation notices for your address. Leave promptly when ordered, using the current official route; do not delay for possessions. If a fire threatens your escape or you are trapped, call emergency services with your precise location.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Confirm your zone

    Use fire agency and emergency management alerts rather than old perimeter maps.

  2. 02

    Gather people and pets

    Take essential medicines, identification, keys and phone power without delaying departure.

  3. 03

    Use official routes

    Smoke and closures can change quickly; do not drive into heavy smoke or flames.

  4. 04

    Report your destination

    Tell an out-of-area contact where you are going and follow shelter instructions.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Leave before the only usable route is threatened. If no official update is reachable, act on visible nearby fire, rapidly worsening conditions and verified local information rather than waiting for an order. Do not drive into smoke with unknown visibility or through flames.

People & practical needs.

Assign one person to dependents and pets and another to transport and contacts. Keep essential medicine, access aids and car keys together. Agree on a reunion place that is outside the threatened area, with a second option if access changes.

Keep essentials within reach

Prioritize supplies named in this guide that are usable by the person who needs them. Keep the checklist and relevant contact details accessible. Check carrying capacity, storage and equipment instructions in advance; collecting equipment must not delay the immediate protective action.

Next steps.

Return only when reentry is allowed. Check for damaged utilities, hot debris and continuing smoke.

Common questions.

When should a household with access needs leave?

Arrange transport, medicine, pets and equipment before routes become crowded or smoky. Leaving early can reduce the difficulty of moving a person who needs extra time; verify the route and destination rather than relying on a single notification.

Can a respirator make a road safe?

No. A particle respirator does not protect against heat, every gas or a blocked road. Smoke protection is one part of the decision; active fire, access and changing wind can make travel unsafe regardless of a mask.

Watch for.

A smoke-control room cannot protect you from a fire approaching the building.
