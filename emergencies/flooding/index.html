OZ FIELD GUIDE / 04 / FLOOD

Flooding

Distance from floodwater is the first safety decision.

UPDATED 02 OCT 2026ABOUT 3 MIN READ

THE QUICK ANSWER

Move to higher ground if water is rising or a flash-flood warning affects you. Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater; depth, current, contamination, and road damage are hard to judge. Follow evacuation orders and avoid basements or low areas that can fill rapidly.

!

In immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Current instructions for your location take priority.

01 / DO NOW

First moves.

  1. 01

    Get above the water

    Leave low ground and move to a safe higher location. If a road is flooded, turn around and choose another route.

  2. 02

    Read the local notice

    Check alerts, road closures, and evacuation instructions. Do not rely on the road being open because it was open earlier.

  3. 03

    Keep away from hazards

    Floodwater may hide energized wires, debris, sewage, chemicals, and missing road surface. Keep children and pets away.

  4. 04

    If trapped, call for help

    Give your precise location to emergency services. Move to a higher level if possible; avoid entering a closed attic where you could become trapped by rising water.

02 / NEXT

When the first minute is over.

Return only when officials say it is safe. Photograph damage without entering unstable structures, use protective gear for cleanup, and check the water supply for advisories.

03 / WATCH FOR

The exception that matters.

Flooding can begin far from the heaviest rain. Do not attempt to cross flowing water to reach a vehicle, home, or belongings.

Sources & context.
