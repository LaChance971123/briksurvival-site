Boil-water advisory

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Use bottled water or bring clear tap water to a rolling boil for 1 minute, then cool it; above 6,500 feet, boil for 3 minutes. Use approved water for drinking, cooking and brushing teeth. Follow the exact local notice. Boiling does not remove chemical contamination.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Confirm the notice

    Check that it is a boil-water advisory, rather than do-not-drink or do-not-use.

  2. 02

    Boil and cool

    Use the local authority instructions; allow water to cool safely before use.

  3. 03

    Prevent accidental use

    Avoid connected refrigerator water and ice; use safe water for food, formula and pets.

  4. 04

    Follow the all-clear

    Resume normal use only after official clearance and any flushing instructions.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

If fuel or electricity is unavailable, use sealed drinking water first. A treatment tablet is an option only for microbial risk and only when its label covers the conditions. If you cannot establish whether chemicals are involved, change the water source rather than experimenting.

People & practical needs.

Keep one clearly marked clean-water supply for drinking, brushing teeth, food and medicine. Ask the care team about medical devices requiring special water. Powdered formula is not sterile; feeding plans need extra care for young or vulnerable infants.

Keep essentials within reach

Prioritize supplies named in this guide that are usable by the person who needs them. Keep the checklist and relevant contact details accessible. Check carrying capacity, storage and equipment instructions in advance; collecting equipment must not delay the immediate protective action.

Next steps.

Keep bottled water and a safe means of boiling available. If boiling is impractical, use the alternative named by officials.

Common questions.

Does my filter remove the need to boil?

Do not assume it does. Household filters may not remove all germs, and some do not address the contaminant involved. Follow the actual advisory and the linked CDC instructions; use bottled water when boiling cannot be done safely.

How do I avoid recontamination?

Cool treated water safely and store in a clean, covered food-grade container. Keep dirty utensils and untreated water away from it. Mark the container and use a clean method to pour or dispense.

Watch for.

A household pitcher filter does not replace boiling during this advisory.
