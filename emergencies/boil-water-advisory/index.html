Boil-water advisory

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Use bottled water or bring clear tap water to a rolling boil for 1 minute, then cool it; above 6,500 feet, boil for 3 minutes. Use approved water for drinking, cooking and brushing teeth. Follow the exact local notice. Boiling does not remove chemical contamination.

Take it with you. Verify locally.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Confirm the notice

    Check that it is a boil-water advisory, rather than do-not-drink or do-not-use.

  2. 02

    Boil and cool

    Use the local authority instructions; allow water to cool safely before use.

  3. 03

    Prevent accidental use

    Avoid connected refrigerator water and ice; use safe water for food, formula and pets.

  4. 04

    Follow the all-clear

    Resume normal use only after official clearance and any flushing instructions.

Next steps.

Keep bottled water and a safe means of boiling available. If boiling is impractical, use the alternative named by officials.

Watch for.

A household pitcher filter does not replace boiling during this advisory.
