Vehicle breakdown

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Move out of traffic if you safely can, switch on hazard lights and call roadside assistance or emergency services for immediate danger. Give your road, direction and nearest marker. On a high-speed road, follow local road-authority advice about whether to stay belted inside or exit to a protected area.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Protect from traffic

    Do not stand in travel lanes or between vehicles. Avoid repairs beside moving traffic.

  2. 02

    Give a precise location

    Share the road name, direction, marker and number of people.

  3. 03

    Assess immediate danger

    If fire, smoke or a collision threat makes the vehicle unsafe, move to a safe location away from traffic when possible.

  4. 04

    Wait safely

    Keep children and pets protected and conserve phone power. In remote areas do not wander without a known safe destination.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Record your exact location, stay visible without standing in traffic and conserve your phone. Do not begin walking along a dangerous road or into remote terrain without a known reachable refuge. In heat or cold, watch occupants closely and reassess whether the vehicle remains safe.

People & practical needs.

Count occupants, monitor temperature and keep children away from traffic. Preserve medicines, water and a light within reach. Do not split the group without a clear safe destination and a reliable way to reconnect.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, drinking water, a battery light, shoes, phone power and a written contact card within reach. Adapt the load to the hazard and the people with you; do not delay escape to collect equipment.

Next steps.

Carry water, weather protection, a charged phone and a vehicle kit. Check tires, fuel or charge before travel.

Watch for.

Exit choices depend on the road and surroundings; do not apply one universal rule to every breakdown.
