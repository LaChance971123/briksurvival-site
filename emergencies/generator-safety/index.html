Generator safety

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Operate a portable generator outdoors at least 20 feet from doors, windows and vents, with exhaust directed away from buildings. Never run it in a home, garage or enclosed space. Use working CO alarms and never connect a generator to a wall outlet.

Take it with you. Verify locally.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Choose outdoor placement

    Keep the generator away from occupied buildings and openings, including neighbors.

  2. 02

    Connect safely

    Use rated cords as instructed; a building connection requires a professionally installed transfer switch.

  3. 03

    Refuel safely

    Shut down and let it cool before refueling. Store fuel safely outside living areas.

  4. 04

    Check CO alarms

    If an alarm sounds or symptoms occur, leave for fresh air and call emergency services.

Next steps.

Read the manufacturer instructions before a crisis. Plan weather protection that does not enclose the generator or obstruct exhaust.

Watch for.

An open garage door does not make indoor generator operation safe.
