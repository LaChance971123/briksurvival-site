Do now.

  1. 01

    Choose shelter before the next attack

    Prefer a designated protective shelter if safely reachable. Otherwise use the most protective intact interior area available, away from glazing. Consider collapse, fire and flooding as well as blast exposure; no ordinary room guarantees safety.

  2. 02

    Keep essentials beside you

    Gather a battery light, footwear, safe water, medicines and written contacts without exposing yourself to retrieve them. Keep corridors and exits clear.

  3. 03

    Make a family check-in plan

    Choose nearby and out-of-area reunion contacts. Use short private messages with your location and status; do not publish live movements or other people’s locations.

  4. 04

    Reassess before moving

    Confirm a destination and route as recently as possible with independent trusted information. A claimed corridor or ceasefire is not proof of safe passage. Do not cross active fighting.

  5. 05

    Avoid explosive debris

    Leave unfamiliar objects, wires, fragments and abandoned munitions untouched. Warn people from a safe position and use verified demining or humanitarian channels when safe.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Choose the most protective reachable room before another attack, keep footwear and a light there, and keep essential medicines close. Do not cross active fighting to collect supplies. Use verified humanitarian contacts and trusted people; government assistance or safe passage may not exist.

People & practical needs.

Choose two reunion places and an out-of-area contact. Keep children with a trusted caregiver and carry a written contact card. Share sensitive addresses and travel details only with people who need them; avoid posting live locations or images of other families without consent. Next steps.

Inventory water, food, medical supplies and power for the people with you. Arrange care tasks and check-ins with trusted neighbours where safe. Verify humanitarian assistance directly; do not assume government, embassy or aid transport will arrive.
