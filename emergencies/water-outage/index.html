Water outage

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Check the utility notice to learn whether water service is interrupted and whether an advisory applies. Use stored or commercially bottled water for drinking and essential care. Service returning does not automatically mean tap water is safe.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Find the notice

    Check the affected address, restoration estimate and any advisory.

  2. 02

    Prioritize safe water

    Reserve approved water for drinking, medications, formula, food and pets.

  3. 03

    Plan hygiene

    Use local health instructions for sanitation and safe handwashing without running water.

  4. 04

    Wait for clearance

    Follow flushing and treatment instructions once the utility restores service.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Inventory clean containers and safe water, then prioritise drinking, food, medicines and hand hygiene. Do not use nonpotable water in food or formula. Plan toilet and waste arrangements separately and keep them away from food and water storage.

People & practical needs.

Write down water, food, battery and medication needs for each person. Keep clean and dirty containers separate and preserve essential power before entertainment. Agree on the point at which lack of water, medical support or safe temperature means you must relocate.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, drinking water, a battery light, shoes, phone power and a written contact card within reach. Adapt the load to the hazard and the people with you; do not delay escape to collect equipment.

Next steps.

Build an emergency water supply and know how your household will handle sanitation.

Watch for.

Do not use heating-system or radiator water for drinking.
