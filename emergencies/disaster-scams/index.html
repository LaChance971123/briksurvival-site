Disaster scams & false aid offers

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Pause before paying, sharing identification or following an urgent aid message. Verify the organisation using a contact you find independently, ask for written terms and avoid pressure to pay immediately. Keep emergency funds for verified essentials. A logo, uniform or forwarded link does not establish trust.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Find an independent contact

    Use the organisation’s known website or an existing trusted number rather than the contact in an unsolicited message. Confirm the specific programme or provider.

  2. 02

    Protect personal information

    Share only what the verified process needs. Never give account passwords, one-time codes or a full identity document to an unverified caller.

  3. 03

    Check payment demands

    Unexpected upfront fees, gift cards, cryptocurrency or pressure to pay immediately are warning signs. Ask for written terms and time to review.

  4. 04

    Check repair offers

    Verify relevant credentials, references and a written scope before hiring. Do not let a door-to-door offer rush you into unsafe entry or a large advance payment.

  5. 05

    Preserve evidence safely

    Keep receipts and message details without engaging further. Use verified consumer-protection or legal support when safe and available.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

If reporting channels are unavailable, stop contact, protect remaining money and accounts, and retain the records for later. A promised refund is not a reason to make another payment.

People & practical needs.

Assign a trusted adult to each person who needs assistance. Keep medicines, essential contacts and a workable reunion plan with the group. Make decisions around the least mobile person, not the fastest; avoid splitting the family without a clear, safe way to reconnect.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, safe drinking water, a battery light, footwear, phone power and written contacts accessible. Carry only what you can manage; collecting supplies must not delay escape.

Next steps.

Warn trusted people privately about confirmed scams without circulating sensitive victim information. If payment or credentials were compromised, contact the relevant bank or provider through a known channel.

Watch for.

An offer of transport or shelter may also carry exploitation risk. Do not surrender documents or separate from family because a stranger promises priority assistance.
