PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

If you live in a surge zone, arrange an inland destination before the storm. Do not wait for public transport that has not been confirmed. Once roads are flooded, do not force a crossing; use the safest elevated location and signal if trapped.

People & practical needs.

Account for children, older people, pets and anyone who needs assistance before moving. Keep shoes, medicines and a torch in the shelter area. Decide who helps whom and who carries the essential supplies; do not send a lone child to retrieve items. For this situation, brief every person on the first action: check the surge zone.
