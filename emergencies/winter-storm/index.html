Winter storm

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Stay indoors and postpone travel during dangerous snow, ice or blowing snow. Keep heat safe, use battery lighting and watch for power loss. If stranded in a vehicle, stay with it unless a safer shelter is clearly accessible.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Check road conditions

    Use official closures and forecasts before deciding to travel.

  2. 02

    Keep warmth safe

    Wear dry layers; never heat a home with an oven, grill or indoor generator.

  3. 03

    Prepare for outages

    Keep essential medicines, water, food, light and charged batteries accessible.

  4. 04

    If stranded

    Call for help with your location. If running the engine briefly for heat, first clear snow from the exhaust and crack a window; monitor for carbon monoxide.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Keep dry layers, safe food, water and battery light together. If stranded in a vehicle, keep the exhaust clear before running the engine and conserve fuel; do not sleep with the engine running. Remain with the vehicle when walking would expose you to poor visibility or severe cold.

People & practical needs.

Account for children, older people, pets and anyone who needs assistance before moving. Keep shoes, medicines and a torch in the shelter area. Decide who helps whom and who carries the essential supplies; do not send a lone child to retrieve items.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, drinking water, a battery light, shoes, phone power and a written contact card within reach. Adapt the load to the hazard and the people with you; do not delay escape to collect equipment.

Next steps.

Check on people who rely on powered equipment or may lack safe heat. Arrange relocation before indoor temperatures become dangerous.

Watch for.

Do not walk away from a stranded vehicle in poor visibility unless you know a safe nearby destination.
