Do now.

  1. 01

    Notice warning signs

    New ground cracks, tilting trees, unusual runoff or rumbling warrant immediate caution and a call to local authorities.

  2. 02

    Leave the affected area

    Use an official safe route rather than following a drainage channel.

  3. 03

    Avoid crossings

    Mud and debris can sweep away vehicles and hide damaged road surfaces.

  4. 04

    Report from safety

    Give the location and visible hazards to emergency services; keep others out.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Stay away from channels, fresh debris and unstable slopes after the first movement. Do not cross the slide to check a house or search for belongings. Choose a destination outside the runout area rather than simply a higher point on the same unstable slope.

People & practical needs.

Account for children, older people, pets and anyone who needs assistance before moving. Keep shoes, medicines and a torch in the shelter area. Decide who helps whom and who carries the essential supplies; do not send a lone child to retrieve items. For this situation, brief every person on the first action: notice warning signs. Agree who will manage children, pets or mobility needs while that action happens. Another slide can follow the first, especially during continued rain.

Keep essentials within reach

For landslide & debris flow, prioritize only items needed for the first action and the fallback: Stay away from channels, fresh debris and unstable slopes after the first movement. Do not cross the slide to check a house or search for belongings. Choose a destination outside the runout area rather than simply a higher point on the same unstable slope. Keep the relevant checklist and contact information within reach. Equipment retrieval must not delay the protective step.

Next steps.

Return only after local authorities assess stability. Contact a qualified professional about slope or structural damage.

Common questions.

What matters before collecting supplies?

Move away from a slide area if you can do so safely, and obey evacuation warnings. Avoid steep slopes, channels and places downhill from recent burns during heavy rain. Do not cross moving debris or enter a fresh slide to inspect it. Collect items only if doing so does not delay this action.

What should another household member do?

Use a second person to handle communication or dependents while the first carries out the immediate action. For landslide & debris flow, keep the specific hazard in view: Another slide can follow the first, especially during continued rain.

What should be written down?

Record when the situation changed, what action was taken and what remains uncertain. For this situation, start with: Report from safety. Give the location and visible hazards to emergency services; keep others out. Keep personal notes private; they are not submitted to Osprey Zero.
