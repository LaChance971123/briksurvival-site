Emergency guide

Natural gas leak

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

If you smell gas or suspect a leak, leave the area on foot immediately. Do not use switches, flames, phones or engines in the suspected leak area. Once safely away, call emergency services and your gas utility. Do not reenter until cleared.

Take it with you. Verify locally.

Do nowNextWatch forSources

Do now.

  1. 01

    Leave now

    Do not search for the leak or stop to collect belongings.

  2. 02

    Avoid ignition

    Do not turn electrical devices on or off, light flames, operate garage doors or start a vehicle nearby.

  3. 03

    Call from safety

    Give the address and what you noticed to emergency services and the utility.

  4. 04

    Keep others clear

    Wait outside the danger area and follow responder instructions.

Next steps.

Have the utility or a qualified technician restore service and inspect appliances when required.

Watch for.

Never use a flame to test for leaks or attempt your own repair.
