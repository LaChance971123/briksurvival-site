Emergency guide

Natural gas leak

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

If you smell gas or suspect a leak, leave the area on foot immediately. Do not use switches, flames, phones or engines in the suspected leak area. Once safely away, call emergency services and your gas utility. Do not reenter until cleared.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Leave now

    Do not search for the leak or stop to collect belongings.

  2. 02

    Avoid ignition

    Do not turn electrical devices on or off, light flames, operate garage doors or start a vehicle nearby.

  3. 03

    Call from safety

    Give the address and what you noticed to emergency services and the utility.

  4. 04

    Keep others clear

    Wait outside the danger area and follow responder instructions.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Stay outside and away even if the utility line is busy. Keep others from entering without approaching the leak. A lack of response does not make reentry safe; use another place to stay until the hazard has been assessed by qualified personnel.

People & practical needs.

Choose an outside meeting place and check that every person can use the escape route. Keep keys, mobility aids and shoes accessible. Tell neighbours about a hazard from a safe location; do not organise an untrained entry or rescue.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, drinking water, a battery light, shoes, phone power and a written contact card within reach. Adapt the load to the hazard and the people with you; do not delay escape to collect equipment.

Next steps.

Have the utility or a qualified technician restore service and inspect appliances when required.

Watch for.

Never use a flame to test for leaks or attempt your own repair.
