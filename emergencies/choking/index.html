Emergency guide

Adult & child choking

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

If someone cannot speak, breathe or cough effectively, call emergency services. For a responsive adult or child, give 5 back blows followed by 5 abdominal thrusts, repeating until the blockage clears or they become unresponsive. Use chest thrusts when pregnant or when you cannot encircle the abdomen.

Take it with you. Verify locally.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Assess the cough

    If the person can cough effectively, encourage coughing and watch closely.

  2. 02

    Call for help

    Send someone to call emergency services, or use speakerphone without delaying care.

  3. 03

    Give back blows and thrusts

    Lean them forward for 5 back blows between shoulder blades, then give 5 abdominal thrusts; follow dispatcher guidance.

  4. 04

    If unresponsive

    Lower them safely and start CPR as directed. Remove only a clearly visible object; never blindly sweep the mouth.

Next steps.

Get medical assessment after thrusts or persistent symptoms. Learn separate infant choking care.

Watch for.

Do not use abdominal thrusts on an infant under one year; infant care uses back blows and chest thrusts.
