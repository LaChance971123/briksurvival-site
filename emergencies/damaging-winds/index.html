Damaging winds

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Shelter inside a sturdy building away from windows when damaging winds threaten. Avoid trees, power lines and lightweight structures. Follow tornado warnings separately; straight-line winds can also cause severe damage.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Secure items beforehand

    Bring loose items inside while conditions are calm; do not go out once winds are dangerous.

  2. 02

    Use an interior room

    Stay away from glass and large exterior openings.

  3. 03

    Limit driving

    High-profile vehicles are vulnerable; avoid routes lined with falling trees or debris.

  4. 04

    Keep clear afterward

    Assume downed lines are live and report them from a safe distance.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Wait for winds to ease in the best shelter you can reach without exposure. Keep a torch and shoes nearby for broken glass. If the building is failing, move to a safer reachable space without going beneath trees, loose roofing or wires.

People & practical needs.

Account for children, older people, pets and anyone who needs assistance before moving. Keep shoes, medicines and a torch in the shelter area. Decide who helps whom and who carries the essential supplies; do not send a lone child to retrieve items. For this situation, brief every person on the first action: secure items beforehand. Agree who will manage children, pets or mobility needs while that action happens. Do not attempt to clear a tree from a power line.

Keep essentials within reach

For damaging winds, prioritize only items needed for the first action and the fallback: Wait for winds to ease in the best shelter you can reach without exposure. Keep a torch and shoes nearby for broken glass. If the building is failing, move to a safer reachable space without going beneath trees, loose roofing or wires. Keep the relevant checklist and contact information within reach. Equipment retrieval must not delay the protective step.

Next steps.

Inspect damage without entering unstable buildings. Arrange help for trees touching wires.

Watch for.

Do not attempt to clear a tree from a power line.
