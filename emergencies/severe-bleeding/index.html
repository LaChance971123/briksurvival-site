Severe bleeding

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Call emergency services and press firmly directly on the bleeding wound with a dressing or clean cloth. Maintain pressure. For life-threatening bleeding from an arm or leg, use a commercial tourniquet if trained; follow dispatcher instructions.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Protect and call

    Check scene safety, use gloves if available and get emergency help.

  2. 02

    Apply direct pressure

    Press firmly over the wound and keep pressing until bleeding stops or appropriate help takes over.

  3. 03

    Use trained techniques

    Use a tourniquet or wound packing for the appropriate injury if trained, following the device and dispatcher instructions.

  4. 04

    Monitor the person

    Keep them from getting cold, check breathing and remain until responders arrive.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Maintain firm pressure and have another person arrange the safest reachable medical care. A tourniquet, if indicated and used, must stay in place until a medical professional takes over; note its application time. Do not keep releasing pressure to check whether bleeding has stopped.

People & practical needs.

Give someone the specific task of calling or locating care while another person provides first aid. Keep the patient warm or cool as the condition requires and record what happened and when. Do not put yourself into the same hazard or assume an ambulance has been dispatched until confirmed.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, drinking water, a battery light, shoes, phone power and a written contact card within reach. Adapt the load to the hazard and the people with you; do not delay escape to collect equipment.

Next steps.

Get urgent medical assessment even if bleeding stops. Take a practical bleeding-control course.

Watch for.

Do not pull an embedded object out; press around it and get help.
