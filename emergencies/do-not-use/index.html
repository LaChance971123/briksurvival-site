Do-not-use advisory

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Do not drink or use tap water for any purpose while a do-not-use advisory is in effect. Avoid contact with skin, eyes and lungs. Use commercially bottled or officially approved alternatives and follow local instructions for sanitation.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Stop tap-water use

    Do not bathe, wash dishes or use connected water and ice appliances.

  2. 02

    Get safe alternatives

    Check official water distribution and hygiene arrangements.

  3. 03

    Prevent accidental exposure

    Tell everyone in the household and keep children and pets from using taps.

  4. 04

    Follow restoration steps

    Wait for the official all-clear and complete any required flushing or appliance instructions.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Make the unsafe supply physically obvious with signs and tell every person in the household. Use a separate clean-water container for essential washing. Avoid flushing or other contact unless the advisory specifically permits it; find another sanitation arrangement.

People & practical needs.

Write down water, food, battery and medication needs for each person. Keep clean and dirty containers separate and preserve essential power before entertainment. Agree on the point at which lack of water, medical support or safe temperature means you must relocate.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, drinking water, a battery light, shoes, phone power and a written contact card within reach. Adapt the load to the hazard and the people with you; do not delay escape to collect equipment.

Next steps.

Plan temporary access to safe washing and sanitation if restrictions continue.

Watch for.

Boiling does not override a do-not-use order.
