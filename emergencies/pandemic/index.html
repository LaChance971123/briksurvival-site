Do now.

  1. 01

    Find current guidance

    Use the local health department and CDC disease-specific updates.

  2. 02

    Reduce transmission

    Improve cleaner indoor air and use recommended precautions for the way the disease spreads.

  3. 03

    Plan care

    Ask your clinician about risk, treatment access and continuity of essential medicines.

  4. 04

    Know urgent signs

    Seek emergency help for severe breathing difficulty, new confusion or other life-threatening symptoms.

Next steps.

Arrange sick-leave, caregiver and household supply plans. Verify information before sharing it.

Watch for.

Do not transfer isolation durations or treatment advice from one disease to another.
