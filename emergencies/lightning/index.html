Lightning

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

When you hear thunder, move into a substantial building or enclosed hard-top vehicle immediately. There is no safe place outdoors during a thunderstorm. Wait at least 30 minutes after the last thunder before going back out.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Leave exposed areas

    Get out of water and off ridges, fields and beaches. Do not shelter beneath a lone tree.

  2. 02

    Choose real shelter

    Use a building or enclosed hard-top vehicle; picnic shelters and tents are not adequate.

  3. 03

    Avoid indoor pathways

    Stay away from showers, sinks, corded phones and plugged-in equipment.

  4. 04

    Help a struck person

    Call emergency services. A lightning victim carries no electrical charge; give CPR if needed and follow dispatcher instructions.

Next steps.

Check forecasts before outdoor activity and identify shelter before leaving it.

Watch for.

Lightning can strike beyond rainfall. Do not wait for rain to seek shelter.
