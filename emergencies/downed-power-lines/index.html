Do now.

  1. 01

    Stay back

    Do not approach to determine whether a wire is live. Current can travel through wet ground and conductive objects. Keep others and animals away.

  2. 02

    Avoid connected objects

    Do not touch fences, trees, vehicles or debris that may contact a wire. Do not use a stick or tool to move it.

  3. 03

    Stay out of flooded electrical areas

    Never enter water to reach a breaker, appliance or person near damaged electrical systems. Use qualified help.

  4. 04

    Warn from safety

    Tell people to avoid the area from your safe location. Report a clear location without approaching for a photograph.

  5. 05

    Do not restore damaged systems

    Leave flooded, scorched or broken equipment off until qualified personnel assess it. A restored grid does not make damaged wiring safe.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Keep the hazard avoided if the utility cannot be reached. Do not move a wire to open a road or restore access; arrange a different safe route.

People & practical needs.

Assign a trusted adult to each person who needs assistance. Keep medicines, essential contacts and a workable reunion plan with the group. Make decisions around the least mobile person, not the fastest; avoid splitting the family without a clear, safe way to reconnect. For this situation, brief every person on the first action: stay back. Agree who will manage children, pets or mobility needs while that action happens. Do not assume a fixed visible distance proves safety. Lines may reenergise without warning; no spark or hum is not an all-clear.

Next steps.

If a wire falls onto a vehicle you occupy, generally stay inside and call for help; fire or another immediate threat changes the decision. Follow qualified emergency instructions rather than touching the vehicle and ground together.

First decision: Stay back

Do not approach to determine whether a wire is live. Current can travel through wet ground and conductive objects. Keep others and animals away. When the original plan fails

Keep the hazard avoided if the utility cannot be reached. Do not move a wire to open a road or restore access; arrange a different safe route. Reassess the next available location or resource using the warning signs in this guide; do not assume a promised service is operating.

Before changing location

Do not assume a fixed visible distance proves safety. Lines may reenergise without warning; no spark or hum is not an all-clear. Check the actual route and the needs of the person who will have the most difficulty moving. A destination is not confirmed until access and conditions have been checked.
