ABOUT OSPREY ZERO

Knowledge builds
freedom.

Osprey Zero is a place for field knowledge, preparedness, and practical survival intelligence. Our mission is to help individuals and families make practical decisions when services fail, assistance is unavailable or authorities are unsafe to approach. Civilian safety, family continuity and self-reliance guide the library.
