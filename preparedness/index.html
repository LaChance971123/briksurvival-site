Make a plan

Good preparation begins with the people and place you know. Work through a few decisions, then build supplies and skills at a pace that fits your life.

PEOPLE FIRSTPRACTICAL BY DESIGN

THE STARTING FRAMEWORK

Four things to work through.

01 / SITUATION

Know your local risks.

Identify the disruptions most likely where you live and how local authorities send alerts.

02 / PEOPLE

Make a household plan.

Decide how you will reconnect and what each person, pet, or caregiver may need.

03 / ESSENTIALS

Build useful supplies.

Start with what you already have, your budget, and what your household actually uses.

04 / PRACTICE

Learn one skill at a time.

Small, repeatable skills make a plan easier to carry out when conditions change.
