Build your plan.

  1. 01

    Choose an outside-area contact

    Ask a trusted person to help relay status if local calls fail. Confirm what information they should collect and share.

  2. 02

    Record reachable support

    Include household members, caregivers, schools where relevant, veterinary support and essential service contacts. Use current local numbers rather than assuming one number works everywhere.

  3. 03

    Choose two meeting arrangements

    Name a nearby location for an ordinary separation and an outside-area destination for a wider event. Confirm access and acceptance.

  4. 04

    Make usable copies

    Keep a paper card with each person and a protected home copy. Update numbers and care details after a change.

A card should stay small

Record name, trusted contact, meeting point and a few essential support details. Avoid putting full identity documents, access codes, firearm storage locations or detailed medical histories on a card that can be lost. Keep sensitive supporting documents separately with controlled access.

Practice a status message

Use a simple format: safe or needing help, current location, who is present, planned destination and next check-in. An outside contact can relay a consistent message so several household members do not spend battery repeating long calls. Avoid posting private locations publicly.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

Use the agreed meeting plan and written numbers. If calls fail, short texts may be more practical when service becomes available; neither is guaranteed. Do not enter danger to retrieve a phone or make a call.

People & practical needs.

Provide large print, relevant languages or accessible formats. Children need age-appropriate instructions and an adult helper; a card does not replace supervision.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep the equipment and written information described above where the responsible person can reach them. Test access in darkness, with a tired household, and while carrying the items you actually need.

Next steps.

Call or message your chosen contact during ordinary conditions, confirm their role, and replace one outdated number.

Watch for.

A listed contact may be unavailable. Verify destination and care arrangements in advance and keep alternatives.
