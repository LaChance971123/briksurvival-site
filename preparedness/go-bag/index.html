Go bag

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Pack a portable bag with the essentials you need to leave quickly: medicines, documents, contact information, water, food, light, phone power and weather-appropriate clothing. Keep it light enough to carry and a separate grab list for items used every day.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Plan the destination

    Know where you can go and how you will get there.

  2. 02

    Pack personal essentials

    Include medication arrangements, identification copies, keys and accessible supplies.

  3. 03

    Add basic support

    Pack water, snacks, light, charger, hygiene supplies and appropriate clothing.

  4. 04

    Test and maintain

    Carry the bag briefly, reduce excess weight and refresh dated items.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

If you have minutes rather than days, grab essential medicines, documents, shoes, water and a phone charger first. Divide necessary supplies among people who can carry them, without giving children or a person with limited mobility an unmanageable load.

People & practical needs.

Pack for actual infants, pets, access needs and the likely transport mode. Agree which adult carries shared items and who stays with each dependent. Include a destination plan; a bag does not establish that a shelter will admit or support the household.

Keep essentials within reach

Prioritize supplies named in this guide that are usable by the person who needs them. Keep the checklist and relevant contact details accessible. Check carrying capacity, storage and equipment instructions in advance; collecting equipment must not delay the immediate protective action.

When the first option is unavailable

Questions in the field.

Does a stocked bag make leaving easier?

Only if it can be reached and carried while managing dependents. Test the load and remove duplication. Keep essential medicine, documents and mobility aids accessible; do not bury a critical item beneath optional equipment.

What if I cannot take the bag?

Leave when immediate danger requires it. Reaching safety takes priority over retrieving supplies. Keep a short written contact plan with each person and identify an alternate source of essential support ahead of time where possible.

Watch for.

Never delay an urgent evacuation to finish packing.
