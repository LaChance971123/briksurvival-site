Go bag

QUICK ANSWER · START HERE

Pack a portable bag with the essentials you need to leave quickly: medicines, documents, contact information, water, food, light, phone power and weather-appropriate clothing. Keep it light enough to carry and a separate grab list for items used every day.

Do now.

  1. 01

    Plan the destination

    Know where you can go and how you will get there.

  2. 02

    Pack personal essentials

    Include medication arrangements, identification copies, keys and accessible supplies.

  3. 03

    Add basic support

    Pack water, snacks, light, charger, hygiene supplies and appropriate clothing.

  4. 04

    Test and maintain

    Carry the bag briefly, reduce excess weight and refresh dated items.

PLAN AROUND WHAT YOU HAVE

If help is unavailable.

If you have minutes rather than days, grab essential medicines, documents, shoes, water and a phone charger first. Divide necessary supplies among people who can carry them, without giving children or a person with limited mobility an unmanageable load.

People & practical needs.

Build the plan around the least mobile person and the shortest critical supply. Try carrying the kit, opening containers and using the light in the dark. Spend first on safe water, essential medicines, basic food and communication rather than specialised gear you cannot use.

Keep essentials within reach

Keep essential medicines, drinking water, a battery light, shoes, phone power and a written contact card within reach. Adapt the load to the hazard and the people with you; do not delay escape to collect equipment.

Next steps.

Keep bags accessible and plan for pets, infants and mobility equipment.

Watch for.

Never delay an urgent evacuation to finish packing.
