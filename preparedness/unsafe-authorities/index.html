Build your plan.

  1. 01

    Assess the actual threat

    Separate verified incidents and direct threats from rumours. Think about who can access your location or information and whether a call, visit or public post could increase exposure.

  2. 02

    Identify independent support

    Find humanitarian or local legal contacts through their own websites or a trusted existing relationship. Verify a contact through a second channel before sharing sensitive details.

  3. 03

    Limit unnecessary exposure

    Share your location and travel plans only with people who need them. Review location sharing and account access; do not assume an app or a private message guarantees confidentiality.

  4. 04

    Prepare essential records

    Keep identification, medicines, written contacts and copies of necessary documents accessible. Store backups securely without exposing other family members.

  5. 05

    Choose a practical safety plan

    Agree where the household can shelter, who can host you and how to reconnect if separated. Avoid confrontational actions that increase immediate danger.

People & practical needs.

Assign a trusted adult to each person who needs assistance. Keep medicines, essential contacts and a workable reunion plan with the group. Make decisions around the least mobile person, not the fastest; avoid splitting the family without a clear, safe way to reconnect.
