PRIVACY & ADVERTISING

Your information. Your choice.

Updated 2 October 2026. Emergency guides and downloads are free and do not require an account.

Search stays in your browser

Osprey Zero ranks a downloaded search index locally. We do not send searches to an Osprey Zero search service. Typed queries are not added to the URL. Incoming shared search links may contain query text and can remain in browser history or hosting logs. Avoid entering names, addresses or other sensitive personal details.

Email signup

The optional early-access and newsletter forms request an email address. Netlify processes and stores submitted form data for the site operator. Signup collection is active. The selected MailerLite form (Osprey Zero | Early Access) has double opt-in configured but its content and delivery setup are not complete; this release does not promise automated email delivery. Requests made through this site are stored by Netlify until an email connection is verified. Never put emergency reports or personal safety information in a signup form. To stop future messages, use the unsubscribe method in any email you receive.

Ads and third-party services

Browsing, preparedness and field-guide pages automatically load Monetag advertising to support the free library. Ads may use cookies, browser storage, IP addresses and device information for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The vignette zone may display a dismissible advertising overlay. The separate push zone can offer advertising notifications on browsing hubs; it requires browser permission. Advertising is separate from our guidance and is not an endorsement.

The homepage, search, urgent response pages, privacy page and signup confirmation do not initialise ad zones. On other eligible pages, third-party ad code loads automatically, using the vignette zone. Browsing hubs also load a separate push-subscription zone. Disaster-scam and information-verification articles are eligible reading pages; other emergency articles stay ad-free. There is currently no site control to disable ads and no paid ad-free plan. Ad appearance and frequency are controlled by the provider and browser.

Push notifications require browser permission. If you have previously allowed advertising notifications, revoke that permission in your browser’s site settings; the uploaded advertising service worker is not an offline guide cache or an emergency alert service.

Browser storage and hosting

Osprey Zero no longer reads or saves the former focus-mode and advertising-session preferences. Advertising providers may use their own cookies and browser storage. Netlify may retain request and security logs. Site fonts are hosted locally. Outside references and advertising services have their own privacy practices.

Offline storage

Saving the offline reader stores complete guide text and fonts in this browser. Use Remove saved reader to delete that cache. The reader has a separate worker scoped to /offline/ and does not replace the advertising worker. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; downloaded PDFs and ZIP files remain until deleted on the device. Household worksheets are private files and are not submitted to the site.

Corrections and privacy requests

Report an editorial correction through the project issue form. This is public: do not include names, locations, email addresses or other sensitive details. For private requests, use the site operator contact supplied with any email received; a dedicated private contact channel is still awaiting configuration.

Safer browsing in a crisis

Do not share live family locations or sensitive identity information through public links. Saving a checklist before an outage reduces your reliance on a network. For a rights or privacy concern, seek a trusted local legal or independent support channel when safe.
