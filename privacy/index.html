PRIVACY & ADVERTISING

Your information. Your choice.

Updated 2 October 2026. Emergency guides and downloads are free and do not require an account.

Search stays in your browser

Osprey Zero ranks a downloaded search index locally. We do not send searches to an Osprey Zero search service. On the search page, query text is part of the URL so it can be shared; it can remain in browser history and hosting logs. Avoid entering names, addresses or other sensitive personal details.

Email signup

The optional early-access and newsletter forms request an email address. Netlify processes and stores submitted form data for the site operator. This release collects signup requests; automated newsletter delivery is not configured by this update. Never put emergency reports or personal safety information in a signup form. To stop future messages, use the unsubscribe method in any email you receive.

Ads and third-party services

Browsing, preparedness and field-guide pages automatically load Monetag advertising to support the free library. Ads may use cookies, browser storage, IP addresses and device information for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Monetag MultiTag may display overlays, open advertising tabs or offer browser push notifications. Advertising is separate from our guidance and is not an endorsement.

The homepage, search, urgent response pages, privacy page and signup confirmation do not initialise ad zones. On other eligible pages, third-party ad code loads automatically, with one zone per page. There is currently no site control to disable ads and no paid ad-free plan. Ad appearance and frequency are controlled by the provider and browser.

Push notifications require browser permission. If you have previously allowed advertising notifications, revoke that permission in your browser’s site settings; the uploaded advertising service worker is not an offline guide cache or an emergency alert service.

Browser storage and hosting

Osprey Zero no longer reads or saves the former focus-mode and advertising-session preferences. Advertising providers may use their own cookies and browser storage. Netlify may retain request and security logs. The site also uses externally hosted Google Fonts and links to outside references; those services have their own privacy practices.

Safer browsing in a crisis

Do not share live family locations or sensitive identity information through public links. Saving a checklist before an outage reduces your reliance on a network. For a rights or privacy concern, seek a trusted local legal or independent support channel when safe.
