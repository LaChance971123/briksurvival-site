PRIVACY & ADVERTISING

Your information. Your choice.

Updated 3 October 2026. The online emergency guides are free to read without an account.

Search stays in your browser

Osprey Zero ranks a search index locally. Typed searches are not sent to an Osprey Zero search service or added to the URL. Incoming shared search links may contain query text in browser history or hosting logs. Avoid entering personal details.

Your reading list

Saved guide links are stored locally in this browser. We store only the selected guide paths, not a downloaded copy of the content. The list has no account or cloud sync and clearing site data removes it. A shared device may expose your saved links. Guides still require an internet connection.

Email requests

The optional toolkit-update and Field Notes forms collect an email address through Netlify. Email delivery is awaiting setup; submitting a request does not promise an immediate message. Never put an emergency report or personal safety information in a signup form. Use the unsubscribe method in any future email to stop messages.

Preparation toolkits and payments

Four topic toolkit editions are complete. Checkout is not currently open; product pages show their contents and edition, with pricing confirmed before sales begin. Checkout and purchased-file delivery will take place through Payhip and its payment providers. Osprey Zero will not collect card details or host purchased documents in this public website.

Payhip privacy information

Household Planner records

The separate Household Planner web launcher verifies and stores a purchased app file locally in this browser. Imported files and household records are not uploaded by the launcher. There is no account or cloud synchronization. Export editable backups regularly: browser storage eviction, clearing site data or removing an installed app can remove local copies. Use shared devices carefully. The planner launch and product pages do not initialise advertising zones.

Ads and third-party services

Browsing, preparedness and field-guide pages automatically load Monetag advertising to support the free encyclopedia. Ads may use cookies, browser storage, IP addresses and device information for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The vignette zone may display a dismissible advertising overlay. A separate push zone can offer advertising notifications on browsing hubs with browser permission. Advertising is separate from our guidance and is not an endorsement.

The homepage, search, urgent response pages, toolkit pages, privacy page and signup confirmation do not initialise ad zones. Other eligible pages load the vignette zone; browsing hubs also load the push-subscription zone. Disaster-scam and information-verification articles are eligible reading pages; other emergency articles stay ad-free. There is no paid ad-free plan or site control to disable ads. Provider and browser settings control ad appearance and frequency.

To revoke previously allowed advertising notifications, use your browser's site settings. The root advertising service worker does not provide emergency alerts.

Browser storage and retired offline access

The former offline reader and free document downloads have retired. When you return to the site, it attempts to remove only the former Osprey Zero offline cache and its worker. Browser restrictions or a device that never reconnects can prevent cleanup. Previously downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them. Fonts are hosted locally. Hosting and advertising services may retain request logs or use their own storage.

Corrections and privacy requests

Report an editorial correction. This is a public issue form: do not include names, locations, email addresses or other sensitive details. For private requests, use the site operator contact supplied with any email received; a dedicated private contact channel is awaiting configuration.

Safer browsing in a crisis

Do not share live family locations or sensitive identity information through public links. Household worksheets in future products are intended to be completed on your own device and kept privately.
