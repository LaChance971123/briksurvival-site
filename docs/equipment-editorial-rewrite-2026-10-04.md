# Equipment guide rewrite

Seven existing routes now provide direct technical explanations rather than repeated crisis-planning language. Five firearm/ammunition pages have six substantial chapters each; night vision and thermal retain their useful chapters with sharper terminology and specific end panels.

- Cartridges: .22 LR/WMR, four common handgun families, .223/5.56, .308/7.62×51/7.62×39, gauges and shotshell labels; sporting/hunting context and documented compatibility.
- Suppressors: separate sound sources, measurement literacy, product categories, material/weight/care considerations and a dated legal-source check. No fixed fee or nationwide conclusion from a court headline.
- Ammunition: packaging, moisture, lot tracking, recalls, inspection and separate flood/fire decisions.
- Firearm fundamentals: parts, action types, chamber versus magazine, controls, maintenance boundaries and stop-use concerns.
- Firearm storage: lock/cabinet/safe comparison, independent ratings, installation, humidity, access and evacuation.
- Observation: intensifier terminology including SNR/resolution/FOM; thermal sensitivity, emissivity, radiometry and display scaling.

Conceptual cartridge, sporting-rifle and sound-source illustrations are inline SVG with captions and accessible text. They contain no assembly instructions or dimensions. Tables retain keyboard-accessible horizontal scrolling and meaningful headers.

Source review includes SAAMI, NSSF/Project ChildSafe, NIOSH, UL, manufacturer documentation and Florida hunting rules. Manufacturer references are labelled; the work is editorial, not independently certified. Legal information remains jurisdiction-dependent and subject to change.
