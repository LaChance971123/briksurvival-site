## Launch sequence

1. Sign in to Payhip in the shared browser; Michael completes payment-provider identity, banking and any binding authorization steps.
2. Connect an appropriate supported payment gateway. Stripe supports a card form; PayPal is an optional additional method. Confirm account readiness rather than assuming connection enables every payment.
3. Confirm store name, support email, currency, final prices and customer-sharing wording. Prepare four digital products as Invisible while reviewing their files and descriptions.
4. Upload only customer ZIPs. Leave the full bundle and standalone planner unreleased.
5. Review receipt text and support instructions. Do not auto-subscribe purchasers to marketing. Do not promise a tax classification or exemption without determining the applicable treatment.
6. Review download limits and customer reset support. Payhip currently documents a default of five attempts. PDF stamping applies to eligible portrait PDFs; do not assume it stamps PDFs inside ZIPs. Buyer-email stamping would also conflict with our sharing-first approach.
7. Use a private 100%-off test coupon and verify checkout, download page, receipt access, complete ZIP extraction, A4/Letter files and Excel. Test orders remain in Payhip's customer records. Separately verify the payment gateway can accept real payments; a free test does not prove this.
8. Record the actual Payhip product IDs, matching checkout URLs, USD prices and edition in content/toolkits.json. Set delivery_verified only after the delivery checks, then status ready. Build checks enforce this boundary.
9. Publish the website Buy links and check each one opens the correct product. Do not redirect purchasers to the site's newsletter /thanks/ page; it is not a purchase confirmation.
